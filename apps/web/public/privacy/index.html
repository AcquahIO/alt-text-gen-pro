Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated 30 August 2026

Images and page context are processed to generate alt text. We do not use them to train our own models or intentionally retain image content after a generation request completes.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Alt Text Generator Pro website, Chrome extension, agent API and MCP service, account system, and related support. “We”, “us”, and “our” mean Alt Text Generator Pro. Privacy questions can be sent to charles@acquah.io.

Information we process

  • Account data: email address, sign-in identifiers, display name, and encrypted authentication information.
  • Image inputs: images selected in the Chrome extension and supported typed image data URLs submitted by an authorised agent. Public image URLs and uploaded asset references are not currently enabled for the agent service.
  • Generation context: product or content title, focus keyword, brand, page title, nearby text, image role, language, and other context you provide.
  • Subscription and access data: Chrome plan, agent-service access, billing status, provider customer and subscription identifiers, and entitlement state. Complete payment card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by us.
  • Operational data: successful generation counts, agent usage and request identifiers, model name, image detail setting, token counts, estimated cost, latency, errors, and timestamps. Generation telemetry does not include the image or page context. An idempotent agent request record may retain its structured response, including generated alt text, so an authorised retry can return the same result without counting it twice.
  • Local extension data: settings, session information, pending uploads, and recent results stored in Chrome storage on your device.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate people and agents, generate and return alt text, apply Chrome and agent entitlements, measure usage, process subscriptions or service charges, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, improve reliability, and respond to support requests. Our legal bases are performance of our contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and compliance with legal obligations.

Service providers and data transfers

Generation inputs are sent to OpenAI to produce alt text. Stripe processes payments and subscription events. Heroku hosts the backend and database; SiteGround hosts the public web experience; Google may process sign-in when you choose Google authentication. These providers may process data outside the UK or EEA under their own safeguards and terms.

Retention

Images and generation context are held for the request and are not intentionally written to our application database. If a processed-file workflow is explicitly enabled in future, short-lived links may be used to return that result. Account, subscription, entitlement, usage-count, connection, and privacy-minimal generation telemetry are retained while access is active and as reasonably needed for billing, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and service reliability. Local Chrome data remains until you clear it, sign out where applicable, or uninstall the extension. Backup copies may persist for a limited period.

Cookies and local storage

We use functional storage for authentication, language choice, saved extension preferences, recent results, and checkout return state. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioural analytics. Stripe and Google may set cookies on their own domains when you use their services.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or an objection to certain processing. You can clear recent results in the product, sign out, or uninstall the extension. Send account or privacy requests to charles@acquah.io. UK users may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Security and children

We use encryption in transit, access controls, scoped entitlements, and standard operational safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The service is intended for business and professional users and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product or law changes. Material changes will be reflected by the date above and, where appropriate, communicated in the product.
